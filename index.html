How it works

Three steps, a few minutes a day.

  1. Check in

    Pick the mood that fits and add a line about your day.

  2. Reflect

    Chat with your companion about whatever came up.

  3. Notice patterns

    Review your week to see what lifts you up and what weighs you down.

A supportive tool, not a substitute for care

Wellness Companion supports everyday reflection. It doesn't diagnose or treat conditions and isn't a replacement for a therapist, doctor, or other qualified professional. If you are in crisis or might hurt yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline right away.
